## Section 2/3 – Standard Lease Terms

**TWO-TIER VALUE-SHARING LEASE©**
**Version 2.1, September 19, 2026**

> *Section 2/3 contains the standard legal provisions for a Two-Tier Value-Sharing Lease©. Capitalized terms used in this Section have the meanings assigned in Sections 1/3 and 3/3. Landlord may revise Sections 2/3 and 3/3 from time to time for clarity. The version of Sections 2/3 and 3/3 identified in Tenant's executed Section 1/3 governs that lease, and a later revision applies to this Lease only if Tenant approves it in writing.*

### Article 1: Rent and Financial Obligations

**1.01 Payment of Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay Base Rent Tier One (Operational Costs) and Base Rent Tier Two (Landlord Investment) monthly in advance on or before the first day of each month during the Term. Rent shall be paid via check or electronic funds transfer (ACH) to an account designated by Landlord. Tenants shall pay all Rent without any prior demand, deduction, set off, or counterclaim. Landlord may designate a successor payment address or account upon written notice.

**1.02 Late Charges and Interest.** If Rent is not received by Landlord within five (5) days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to five percent (5%) of the overdue amount or Fifty Dollars ($50.00), whichever is greater. Any Rent that remains unpaid thirty (30) days after its due date shall also bear interest from that thirtieth day until paid at twelve percent (12%) per annum or the maximum rate permitted by Applicable Laws, whichever is less. Late charges and interest are Additional Rent.

**1.03 CAM Charges (Common Area Maintenance).** Included in Base Rent Tier One, allocated based on each Tenant's Proportionate Share as reasonably determined by Landlord in operating, managing, maintaining, and repairing all Building and Common Areas ("CAM Charges"). CAM Charges include, but are not limited to, the following:

- **(a) Taxes:** All real property and personal property taxes, assessments, King County surface water management fees, and transit taxes.
- **(b) Insurance:** Premiums for Landlord's commercial general liability, property, earthquake, and loss of rents insurance plus any special insurance due to Tenant's business.
- **(c) Maintenance & Common-Area Utilities:** Costs of Common Area services and utilities that are not separately metered or billed to a tenant, including garbage, landscaping, parking lot maintenance, pest control, recycle, sewer, snow/ice removal, trash, waste connections, water, and gas and electricity serving the Common Areas. Utilities that Tenant pays directly under Section 5.01 are not CAM Charges.
- **(d) Management:** A property management fee of five percent (5%), plus the cost of on-site personnel directly involved in operations.
- **(e) Capital Improvements:** The cost of those capital improvements (a) required by new governmental laws (e.g., energy or building code mandates), (b) reasonably intended to reduce or replace CAM Charges, or (c) made to replace, through no fault of Tenant, HVAC or other Building systems serving the Premises or the Common Areas that have reached the end of their useful life, as provided in Section 4.02, in each case amortized over their useful life per GAAP, with 8% interest on any unamortized balance.

**1.04 Exclusions from CAM Charges.** CAM Charges shall not include Landlord marketing expenses, lease commissions, lease-up expenses, political contributions, penalties and fines, legal fees for procuring tenants, Landlord's business income taxes, property debt service, costs related to Landlord's gross negligence, or costs to remediate Hazardous Materials present prior to the Lease Start Date.

**1.05 NNN Variation.** If Section 1/3 - Specific Lease Terms designates this Lease as also Triple Net (NNN), the provisions set forth in that section shall modify or supersede this Article 1 to the extent set forth therein.

**1.06 Estimated Payments and Reconciliation.** Prior to the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall provide a good-faith estimated Annual Budget for Base Rent Tier One for the coming year. Tenant shall pay at least 1/12th of its share monthly into an Escrow Account managed by Landlord. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of the calendar year, or as soon thereafter as reasonably practicable, Landlord shall furnish a reconciliation statement. Tenant shall pay any deficiency within thirty (30) days, and Landlord shall credit any overage to future Base Rent Tier One.

**1.07 Tenant's Audit Rights.** Tenant must submit any audit request within thirty (30) days after receiving the reconciliation statement.

### Article 2: Delivery and Acceptance of Premises

**2.01 Delivery of Possession.** Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver possession of the Premises on or before the Lease Start Date in the condition required by the Lease Terms Sheet.

**2.02 Delayed Possession; Tenant's Cancellation Right.** If Landlord is unable to deliver possession of the Premises in the required delivery condition within sixty (60) days after the Lease Start Date, then (a) Rent shall abate for the period of the delay until possession is delivered, and (b) Tenant may, at its option, cancel this Lease by written notice to Landlord delivered at any time before possession is tendered. Upon such cancellation, Landlord shall promptly refund to Tenant all prepaid Rent and Security Deposit, and neither party shall have any further obligation to the other, except for obligations that expressly survive termination. This Section 2.02 states Tenant's sole remedy for delayed delivery and is subject to extension for Force Majeure and for delays caused by Tenant.

**2.03 Acceptance "As-Is".** Except as expressly stated in the Lease Terms Sheet or a Tenant Work Letter, Tenant accepts the Premises in their then-existing condition upon delivery of possession. Tenant acknowledges that it has had an adequate opportunity to inspect the Premises and that Landlord has made no representations or warranties except as expressly stated in this Lease.

**2.04 Punch List.** If Tenant fails to deliver to Landlord a written list of defects in any Landlord-performed work within thirty (30) days after delivery of possession, Tenant shall be deemed to have accepted the Premises in their then-existing condition. If Tenant timely delivers such a list, Landlord shall correct the listed items that are Landlord's responsibility with reasonable diligence, and the parties shall conduct one inspection-and-cure cycle to confirm completion. Minor defects that do not prevent Tenant from using the Premises for the Permitted Use shall not delay the Rent Commencement Date.

**2.05 Completion Delays Do Not Extend Commencement.** Except as expressly provided in Section 2.02, the time needed to complete punch-list items or any Tenant Improvements shall not delay the Base Rent Tier One Commencement Date, and any delay attributable to Tenant or Tenant's contractors shall not abate or postpone Rent.

### Article 3: Use of Premises and Compliance with Laws

**3.01 Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises solely for the Permitted Use specified in Section 1/3 – Specific Lease Terms and shall operate in a reputable manner. Any change in use requires Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed.

**3.02 Prohibited Uses and Federal Carve-Out.** Notwithstanding any provision of Washington law, Tenant shall not engage in any activity that violates federal law, including the cultivation, distribution, or sale of cannabis, unless expressly authorized by a separate written addendum executed by Landlord.

**3.03 Compliance with Laws and the ADA.** Landlord shall remain responsible for ADA compliance relating to Common Areas and structural elements except to the extent caused by Tenant. Tenant assumes responsibility for complying with all Applicable ADA Laws and bears the cost and responsibility for ensuring that the interior of the Premises, Tenant's operations, and any Alterations comply with the ADA and Washington accessibility codes. Tenant indemnifies Landlord against third-party ADA claims arising from Tenant's interior.

**3.04 Environmental Compliance (Washington MTCA).** Tenant shall strictly comply with the Washington Model Toxics Control Act. Tenant shall not permit Hazardous Materials on the Premises, except for supplies and materials customarily used for the Permitted Use, kept and used in commercially reasonable quantities and in strict compliance with Applicable Laws and manufacturer instructions.

**3.05 Environmental Indemnification.** Tenant shall indemnify, defend, and hold Landlord harmless from all claims, liabilities, damages, and remediation costs arising from Tenant's use, storage, release, or disposal of Hazardous Materials.

### Article 4: Maintenance, Repairs, and Alterations

**4.01 Tenant's Maintenance Obligations.** Tenant shall, at its sole cost, maintain the interior of the Premises (walls, floors, plumbing fixtures, interior glass, and lighting) in clean, safe condition.

**4.02 HVAC.** Landlord shall arrange routine preventive maintenance of HVAC systems serving the Premises. Such costs shall be included in CAM Charges. If replacement is required through no fault of Tenant, Landlord may recover the cost through CAM Charges as a Capital Improvement under Section 1.03, amortized over the useful life of the replacement asset.

**4.03 Landlord's Obligations.** Subject to CAM reimbursement, Landlord shall maintain the structural foundation, load-bearing walls, exterior roof membrane, and Common Areas.

**4.04 Alterations.** Tenant shall not make Alterations without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed for non-structural alterations costing less than $10,000.

**4.05 Mechanic's Liens and Protection.** Tenant shall pay promptly for all labor and materials. Tenant must give Landlord 10 days' written notice before commencing work. Landlord may post notices to protect the property from lien claims. If a lien is filed, Tenant must discharge or bond it within 15 days.

**4.06 Surrender and Abandonment.** Upon expiration or earlier termination, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom-clean and in good condition, Normal Wear and Tear and insured casualty excepted, and shall remove all of Tenant's personal property and Trade Fixtures, repairing any damage caused by removal. Permanent Alterations shall remain Landlord's property unless Landlord, at the time consent was given, conditioned its consent on removal. If Tenant fails to remove its personal property or Trade Fixtures by the end of the Term, such items shall be deemed abandoned, and Landlord may remove, store, sell, or dispose of them at Tenant's expense, without liability and without waiving any other remedy. A final walk-through will be conducted and all keys and access devices returned to Landlord.

### Article 5: Utilities

**5.01 Direct Utilities.** Tenant shall pay directly to the applicable utility provider all charges for utilities separately metered or otherwise attributable to the Premises, as specified in Section 1/3 Lease Terms Sheet (Financial Obligations) (including, by default, electricity, natural gas, telephone, and internet/data service).

**5.02 Shared and Common-Area Utilities.** Where utilities are not separately metered to the Premises (including, by default, water, sewer, trash, and common-area electricity), Tenant shall pay its Proportionate Share as part of CAM Charges, or as otherwise specified in the Lease Terms Sheet or any applicable Annual Budget.

**5.03 Tenant's Obligation to Establish Service.** Tenant shall promptly establish and maintain accounts in Tenant's own name for all direct utilities, and shall keep such accounts current throughout the Term. Tenant shall provide Landlord with evidence of active service upon request. Landlord may request separate copies of all direct utility billings.

**5.04 Interruptions.** Subject to Article 18, Section 18.03 (Utility Interruptions), Landlord shall not be liable for interruptions in utility service except those caused by Landlord's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### Article 6: Insurance and Indemnification

**6.01 Tenant's Required Insurance.** Tenant must carry: $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate Commercial General Liability (CGL); "Special Form" Property Insurance covering 100% of Tenant's property; and Washington State Workers' Compensation coverage as and to the extent required by Applicable Laws for Tenant's employees, if any. If Tenant has no employees, Tenant shall upon request certify that fact to Landlord in writing.

**6.02 Additional Insureds.** CGL policies must name Landlord and Landlord's lender, if any, as Additional Insureds on a primary/non-contributory basis.

**6.03 Mutual Waiver of Subrogation.** Landlord and Tenant mutually waive rights of recovery against each other for any loss covered by the required property insurance.

**6.04 Waiver of Immunity.** Landlord and Tenant each specifically and expressly waive any immunity that each may be granted under the Washington State Industrial Insurance Act, Title 51 RCW. Neither party's indemnity obligations under this Lease shall be limited by any limitation on the amount or type of damages, compensation, or benefits payable to or for any third party under the Worker Compensation Acts, Disability Benefit Acts or other employee benefit acts. LANDLORD AND TENANT ACKNOWLEDGE THAT THE FOREGOING WAIVER WAS SPECIFICALLY AND MUTUALLY NEGOTIATED BY THE PARTIES.

**6.05 General Indemnification.** Tenant shall indemnify, defend, and hold Landlord harmless from claims, damages, liabilities, and expenses arising from Tenant's use of the Premises or breach of this Lease, except to the extent caused by Landlord's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### Article 7: Casualty and Condemnation

**7.01 Casualty.** If damaged by fire or casualty, Landlord shall estimate repair time. If repairable within 180 days, Landlord shall repair the structure, and Base Rent Tier Two abates in proportion to the area or utility of the Premises that Tenant cannot use until the repair is complete. Base Rent Tier One continues and is reconciled to actual costs under Section 1.06. If not repairable within 180 days, Landlord may terminate. Landlord has no duty to repair Tenant's property.

**7.02 Condemnation.** If by exercise of eminent domain (i) more than twenty-five percent (25%) of the rentable floor area of the Premises is taken, or (ii) any portion of the parking, access, or Common Areas is taken in a manner that, in Tenant's reasonable judgment, materially impairs Tenant's ability to operate for the Permitted Use, this Lease shall terminate as of the date of the taking. For any lesser taking, this Lease shall continue, with Base Rent Tier Two equitably abated in proportion to the area or utility lost, applying the same rule as Section 7.01. Base Rent Tier One continues and is reconciled to actual costs under Section 1.06, with any change in Proportionate Share resulting from the taking reflected in the next Annual Budget. Landlord shall receive the entire condemnation award; Tenant may make a separate claim against the condemning authority for Tenant's moving expenses, Trade Fixtures, and loss of business goodwill, only to the extent recoverable independently under Applicable Laws.

### Article 8: Assignment and Subletting

**8.01 Consent.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any portion of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed.

**8.02 Recapture.** Upon Tenant's request to assign or sublet, Landlord has 30 days to elect to recapture the space and terminate the Lease for that portion.

**8.03 Excess Rent.** If Tenant subleases for a profit, 50% of the excess rent (after deducting amortized broker and TI costs) shall be paid to Landlord.

### Article 9: Default and Remedies

**9.01 Events of Default.** Tenant defaults under this Lease if it: (a) fails to pay Rent within five (5) days after required notice; (b) fails to perform a non-monetary obligation within fifteen (15) days after notice; (c) abandons the Premises; or (d) becomes insolvent.

**9.02 Landlord Default.** Landlord shall not be in default unless Landlord fails to perform obligations required of Landlord within thirty (30) days after notice by Tenant to Landlord; provided, however, that if the nature of such default cannot be cured within such thirty (30)-day period, Landlord shall not be in default if Landlord commences such cure within thirty (30) days after notice by Tenant and diligently pursues such cure to completion. If Landlord fails to cure any such default within the allotted time, Tenant's sole remedy shall be to seek actual money damages, but not consequential or punitive damages, for loss arising from Landlord's failure to discharge its obligations under this Lease. Nothing herein shall relieve Landlord from its duty to perform any of its obligations to the standard prescribed in this Lease. Any notice periods granted herein shall run concurrently with, and not in addition to, any default notice periods required by law.

**9.03 Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may exercise any remedy available under this Lease or applicable law. All remedies are cumulative and non-exclusive.

**9.04 Termination of Lease.** Landlord may terminate Tenant's interest under the Lease, but no act by Landlord other than notice of termination from Landlord to Tenant shall terminate this Lease. The Lease shall terminate on the date specified in the notice of termination. Upon termination of this Lease, Tenant will remain liable to Landlord for damages in an amount equal to Rent and other sums that would have been owing by Tenant under this Lease for the balance of the Term, less the net proceeds, if any, of any reletting of the Premises by Landlord subsequent to the termination, after deducting all of Landlord's Reletting Expenses (as defined in Section 3/3). Landlord shall be entitled to either collect damages from Tenant monthly on the days on which Rent or other amounts would have been payable under the Lease, or alternatively, Landlord may accelerate Tenant's obligations under the Lease and recover from Tenant: (i) unpaid Rent which had been earned at the time of termination; (ii) the amount by which the unpaid Rent which would have been earned after termination until the time of award exceeds the amount of Rent loss that Tenant proves could reasonably have been avoided; (iii) the amount by which the unpaid Rent for the balance of the term of the Lease after the time of award exceeds the amount of Rent loss that Tenant proves could reasonably be avoided (discounting such amount by the discount rate of the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco at the time of the award, plus 1%); and (iv) any other amount necessary to compensate Landlord for all the detriment proximately caused by Tenant's failure to perform its obligations under the Lease, or which in the ordinary course would be likely to result from the Event of Default, including without limitation Reletting Expenses described below.

**9.05 Re-Entry and Reletting.** Landlord may continue this Lease in full force and effect, and without demand or notice, re-enter and take possession in accordance with applicable law and pursuant to lawful judicial process.

**9.06 Waiver of Redemption Rights.** Tenant, for itself, and on behalf of any and all persons claiming through or under Tenant, including creditors of all kinds, hereby waives and surrenders all rights and privileges which they may have under any present or future law, to redeem the Premises or to have a continuance of this Lease for the Term, or any extension thereof.

**9.07 Nonpayment of Additional Rent.** All costs that Tenant is obligated to pay to Landlord pursuant to this Lease shall, in the event of nonpayment, be treated as Additional Rent, and Landlord shall have the same rights with respect to nonpayment of Additional Rent as it has with respect to nonpayment of Rent.

**9.08 Failure to Remove Property.** If Tenant fails to remove any of its property from the Premises at Landlord's request following an uncured Event of Default, Landlord may, at its option, remove and store the property at Tenant's expense and risk. If Tenant does not pay the storage cost within fifteen (15) days of Landlord's request, Landlord may, at its option, have any or all of such property sold at public or private sale (and Landlord may become a purchaser at such sale), in such manner as Landlord deems proper, after not less than ten (10) days' prior written notice to Tenant and to any secured party that has filed a financing statement covering the property, and otherwise in the manner required by Applicable Laws. Landlord shall apply the proceeds of such sale: (i) to the expense of such sale, including reasonable attorneys' fees actually incurred; (ii) to the payment of the costs or charges for storing such property; (iii) to the payment of any other sums of money which may then be or thereafter become due Landlord from Tenant under any of the terms hereof; and (iv) the balance, if any, to Tenant. Nothing in this Section shall limit Landlord's right to sell Tenant's personal property as permitted by law or to foreclose Landlord's lien for unpaid Rent, if any.

### Article 10: Subordination, Attornment, and Estoppel Certificates

**10.01 Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any current or future mortgage or ground lease. Tenant shall attorn any successor-in-interest upon foreclosure.

**10.02 Estoppel Certificates.** Within ten (10) business days after written request from Landlord, Tenant shall execute, acknowledge, and deliver to Landlord (or any prospective purchaser, lender or person or entity Landlord designates) a written estoppel certificate certifying, to the extent true, each of the following: (a) that this Lease is unmodified and in full force and effect (or, if modified, stating the modifications); (b) the Lease Start Date, Rent Commencement Date, and Lease Expiration Date; (c) the amount of Base Rent Tier One charges and the date through which each has been paid; (d) the amount of any Security Deposit held by Landlord; (e) that, to Tenant's knowledge, neither party is in default (or specifying any claimed default); (f) that Tenant has accepted and is in possession of the Premises; (g) that there are no uncured Landlord obligations or unapplied concessions, free-rent periods, or allowances (or specifying any); (h) that Tenant has no current offsets, defenses, or claims against Base Rent Tier One, or Base Rent Tier Two, or Additional Rent (or specifying any); and (i) such other factual matters as Landlord, its lender, or a prospective purchaser may reasonably request. Tenant's failure to timely deliver the certificate shall be conclusive that the facts stated in Landlord's proposed form are correct.

### Article 11: Landlord's Right of Entry

**11.01 Landlord may enter upon 24 hours' notice for inspections, repairs, or to show the space.** In emergencies, Landlord may enter without notice.

### Article 12: Relocation of Tenant

**12.01** Landlord may, upon sixty (60) days' notice, relocate Tenant to another suite within the same Building or parcel featuring substantially similar square footage, equivalent visibility, parking, utility capacity, and frontage. Landlord shall pay reasonable out-of-pocket moving and rewiring costs.

### Article 13: Holding Over

**13.01** If Tenant remains in possession after expiration or termination of this Lease without Landlord's written consent, Tenant shall be a holdover tenant at sufferance. Base Rent shall increase to 150% of the rate in effect immediately before expiration during the first thirty (30) days and 200% thereafter. Additional Rent shall continue unchanged. Landlord's acceptance of holdover rent does not create a month-to-month tenancy unless a separate written agreement is signed, and the holdover tenancy at sufferance may be terminated pursuant to Washington law.

### Article 14: Brokers and Agency

**14.01** Broker representations, warranties, and indemnities are set forth in Section 17.03 (No Brokers (Other Than Disclosed)), which applies to both parties.

### Article 15: Notices

**15.01 Delivery.** Legal notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses set forth in Section 1/3 Specific Lease Terms (Notice Addresses), via Personal Delivery, Overnight Courier, or Certified Mail. Notwithstanding anything herein, any notice required under Chapter 59.12 RCW shall be served strictly in the manner required by Washington law (including RCW 59.12.040). Email is invalid for legal default notices.

### Article 16: Dispute Resolution and Waiver of Jury Trial

**16.01 Venue and Fees.** Governed by Washington law. Venue is King County. Prevailing party recovers attorney fees.

**16.02 Waiver of Jury Trial.** TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED, LANDLORD AND TENANT KNOWINGLY, VOLUNTARILY, AND INTENTIONALLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY CIVIL ACTION, PROCEEDING, OR COUNTERCLAIM (INCLUDING UNLAWFUL DETAINER ACTIONS) BROUGHT BY EITHER PARTY AGAINST THE OTHER ON ANY MATTER ARISING OUT OF THIS LEASE.

**17.04 Accuracy of Application Information.** Tenant represents that all financial statements, credit information, and other materials submitted in connection with the application and Lease execution are true and complete in all material respects as of the date submitted.

Tenant shall replenish any credited amount within ten (10) days after demand, and any remaining balance shall be returned within thirty (30) days after surrender.

No member, manager, shareholder, officer, director, trustee, or beneficiary of Landlord shall have personal liability under this Lease. Landlord shall not be liable for consequential, special, or lost-profit damages.

## Section 3/3 – Definitions & Glossary Terms

**TWO-TIER VALUE-SHARING LEASE©**
**Version 2.1, September 19, 2026**

> *This Section 3/3 defines the capitalized terms used throughout the Two-Tier Value-Sharing Lease©, including Section 1/3 (Specific Lease Terms), Section 2/3 (Standard Lease Terms), and Section 3/3 (Definitions & Glossary Terms). Landlord may revise Sections 2/3 and 3/3 from time to time. The revision of Sections 2/3 and 3/3 identified in Tenant's executed Section 1/3 governs this Lease, and a later revision applies to this Lease only if Tenant approves it in writing.*

**1.01 2T-VSL means a Two-Tier Value-Sharing Lease©.** Base Rent Tier One covers the costs of operating, managing, and maintaining the Property. Base Rent Tier Two compensates Landlord for its investment and assumed risk. Together, Tier One and Tier Two constitute Base Rent.

**1.02 Additional Rent.** Any amounts payable by Tenant under the Lease other than Base Rent, including reimbursements, CAM adjustments, interest, late charges, legal fees recoverable under the Lease, indemnity obligations, and other charges expressly designated as Additional Rent

**1.03 Alterations.** Any physical additions, improvement, modification, installation, or alteration to the Premises by the Tenant after the Lease Start Date.

**1.04 Applicable Laws.** All present and future federal, state, county, and municipal laws, ordinances, regulations, and building codes applicable to the Property or Tenants, including all applicable laws, ordinances, regulations, codes, permits and governmental requirements now existing or hereafter enacted.

**1.05 Base Rent:** Tier-One: Operational Costs. The annually fixed, minimum monthly amount Tenant must pay into the Escrow Account for the right to occupy the Premises and Grounds, including but not limited to Tenant share of all CAM Charges, general and special utilities, management fees, equipment reserve fees, and a contingency percentage based on the projected Annual Budget distributed prior to the end of each year. Late charges, interest, and default-related fees shall constitute Additional Rent and not Base Rent.

**1.06 Base Rent:** Tier-Two: Landlord Investment. The fixed or percentage minimum monthly amount Tenant is required to pay to Landlord, at the account Landlord designates under Section 1.01 of Section 2/3, for the right to occupy the Premises, excluding all Base Rent Tier-One Operational Costs.

**1.07 Building.** The entire physical structure and underlying real property in which the Premises are located, including the roof, foundation, exterior walls, and shared utility systems.

**1.08 Building Total Rentable Square Footage.** The total rentable square footage of the Building as stated in Section 1/3 – Specific Lease Terms or, if not stated there, as reasonably determined by Landlord. It is the denominator used to compute Proportionate Share.

**1.09 CAM Charges (Common Area Maintenance).** The costs and expenses incurred by the Landlord to operate, repair, maintain, and manage the Building and the Common Areas. These are allocated on the basis of each Tenant's Proportionate Share as reasonably determined by Landlord.

**1.10 Common Areas.** All areas and facilities outside the Premises that are provided and designated by Landlord for the general, non-exclusive use of Landlord, Tenant, other tenants of the buildings, and their respective employees and invitees. This includes parking lots, landscaped areas, sidewalks, exterior lighting, driveways, and shared trash enclosures.

**1.11 Escrow Account.** A separate account designated by Landlord into which Tenant pays Base Rent Tier One and from which Landlord pays the Operational Costs included in Base Rent Tier One. The Escrow Account is estimated, funded, and reconciled as provided in Section 1.06 of Section 2/3. Any different terms for the Escrow Account stated in Section 1/3 – Specific Lease Terms control.

**1.12 Event of Default.** Any failure or event described in Section 9.01 of Section 2/3 that continues after the notice and cure period, if any, provided there. An Event of Default entitles Landlord to exercise the remedies available under this Lease and applicable law.

**1.13 Good Faith Deposit.** The initial, refundable sum of money submitted by a prospective tenant with its letter of intent or lease application as consideration for Landlord's evaluation, underwriting, lease preparation, and temporary reservation of the Premises.

**1.14 Guarantor.** The individual person (or persons) who signs the Unconditional Guaranty of Lease, making them personally and financially responsible for the Tenant's (the business entity) obligations, debts, and performance under the Lease.

**1.15 Hazardous Materials.** Any substance, chemical, waste, or material defined as toxic, explosive, corrosive, flammable, or hazardous by any federal, state, or local law, rule, or regulation, including the Washington Model Toxics Control Act (MTCA) and CERCLA.

**1.16 Lease Expiration Date.** The specific date on which the Term of the Lease naturally concludes, and the Tenant must surrender the Premises back to the Landlord.

**1.17 Lease Start Date.** The date stated as the Lease Start Date in Section 1/3 – Specific Lease Terms, on which the Term begins and Tenant's right to possession of the Premises commences. The Lease Start Date may precede the Rent Commencement Date to permit Tenant Improvements, fixturing, inspections, setup, or other approved pre-opening activities.

**1.18 Lease Terms Sheet.** The tenant-specific portion of the Lease set forth in Section 1/3, including all exhibits, addenda, guaranties, work letters, and other attachments. Section 1/3 governs Sections 2/3 and 3/3 in the event of a conflict.

**1.19 NNN Lease (Triple Net Lease).** A lease structure, applicable only where Section 1/3 – Specific Lease Terms so elects, under which Tenant pays real property taxes, insurance premiums, and maintenance costs for the Premises directly to the taxing authority, insurer, or vendor, in place of and not in addition to the corresponding CAM Charges that would otherwise be included in Base Rent Tier One. Where elected, the provisions stated for that purpose in Section 1/3 – Specific Lease Terms apply, as provided in Section 1.05 of Section 2/3.

**1.20 Normal Wear and Tear.** The expected minor deterioration that occurs to the Premises simply from the day-to-day, reasonable use of the space over the Term (e.g., minor scuffs on the floor, slight fading of paint). It expressly does not include damages caused by negligence, heavy impact, lack of maintenance, or the removal of Trade Fixtures.

**1.21 Permitted Use.** The strictly defined, specific business activity that the Tenant is allowed to conduct within the Premises, as written in the Section 1/3 – Specific Lease Terms.

**1.22 Premises.** The specific, exclusive physical footprint of space leased to Tenant, usually defined by a suite number, address, or floor plan attached to Section 1/3 – Specific Lease Terms. All square footage figures stated for the Premises are approximate, as determined by Landlord.

**1.23 Proportionate Share.** The percentage of the Building Total Rentable Square Footage assigned to the Premises, as stated in Section 1/3 – Specific Lease Terms. It is used to calculate Tenant's portion of the Base Rent Tier One charges. Stated Proportionate Share percentages are based on approximate measurements and are conclusive for all purposes of this Lease as determined by Landlord.

**1.24 Reletting Expenses.** The costs Landlord reasonably incurs to relet the Premises following an Event of Default, including brokerage commissions, advertising, reasonable attorneys' fees, and the cost of repairs, alterations, and tenant improvements reasonably required to relet the Premises.

**1.25 Rent.** Rent means Base Rent Tier One, Base Rent Tier Two, and Additional Rent.

**1.26 Rent Commencement Date.** The specific date on which the Tenant's obligation to pay applicable Rent first accrues.

**1.27 Security Deposit.** A sum of money paid by Tenant to Landlord upon execution of the Lease and held by Landlord throughout the Term as security for Tenant's performance. Landlord may apply it to damage to the Premises, unpaid Rent, or other defaults by Tenant, and shall hold, apply, and return it as provided in Section 18.01 of Section 2/3. This amount may be commingled with other Landlord funds.

**1.28 Tenant and Subtenant.** The Tenant is the person or business that rents or leases property directly from the property owner (Landlord) and has a legal agreement with the Landlord and is responsible for paying rent and following the lease terms. A Subtenant is the person or business that rents all or part of the leased property from the tenant, rather than directly from the Landlord. This arrangement is called a sublease. The original tenant remains responsible to the landlord for complying with the original lease.

**1.29 Tenant Improvements (TIs).** The initial construction, build-out, or remodeling work required to prepare the "As-Is" space for the Tenant's specific business operations prior to opening.

**1.30 Term.** The total duration of the Lease Agreement, measured from the Lease Start Date to the Lease Expiration Date (including Options).

**1.31 Trade Fixtures.** Items of personal property or equipment installed by the Tenant specifically for the conduct of their business (e.g., commercial espresso machines, specialized retail shelving, portable point-of-sale systems). Unlike Alterations, Trade Fixtures remain the property of the Tenant and must be removed at the expiration of the Lease, provided Tenant repairs any damage caused by their removal.

**1.32 Utilities.** The basic services delivered to the Premises or the Building for use by Tenant or in connection with the operation of the Premises. Utilities generally include garbage/dumpster service, gas, electricity, internet, pest control, recycling, sewer, and waste connections. Any additional services, exceptions, exclusions, or different allocations of responsibility between Landlord and Tenant shall be set forth in Section 1/3 – Specific Lease Terms or an applicable amendment, when identified, which shall control.
